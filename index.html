WHO WE ARE

Building resilient farming communities for a better Rwanda.

BIOHARVEST SOLUTIONS Ltd is a Rwandan company dedicated to restoring degraded soils, promoting Conservation Agriculture Plus (CA+) and strengthening financial inclusion through Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs).

ABOUT BIOHARVEST

Agriculture knowledge alone is not enough.

Farmers also need the financial tools and resources to sustain agricultural transformation.

🌱

Our Vision

A Rwanda where farming communities have healthy, productive soils, practice climate-smart conservation agriculture and enjoy financial security through sustainable savings and credit mechanisms.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower Rwandan farming communities through integrated soil restoration, climate-smart agriculture and financial inclusion.

🇷🇼

Our Roots

BIOHARVEST SOLUTIONS originated from a volunteer community organization founded in 2020 by graduates of the International Paid Agriculture Exchange Program (CAEP) in the United States.

OUR APPROACH

Two powerful solutions. One integrated model.

We combine agricultural knowledge with financial inclusion to help farmers adopt and sustain climate-smart practices.

01
🌾

Conservation Agriculture Plus

CA+ promotes minimum soil disturbance, permanent soil cover and diversified crop rotation to restore soil fertility, retain moisture and improve resilience.

  • Minimum soil disturbance
  • Permanent soil cover
  • Crop rotation
  • Climate-smart farming
02
💰

Village Savings & Loan Associations

VSLAs help farming households mobilize savings, access affordable credit and develop financial management skills.

  • Community savings
  • Affordable agricultural credit
  • Financial literacy
  • Community empowerment
